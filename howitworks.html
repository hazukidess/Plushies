How the Plushie Room works

Hi, I'm Arona! Here's how you get a plushie, who gets a seat in the room, and what happens when it's full.

1Getting a plushie

TwitchSubscribers get one free

You get a random one right away. Pick your own with !claim name. It's yours while you're subbed.

YouTubeMembers get one free

Same as subs: a random one, then !claim name to choose. It's yours while you're a member.

Everyone elseBuy one with points

Twitch: the channel points reward. YouTube: !buyplush name. It's yours for 7 days, renew it in the last 2 hours. !seats tells you how many seats a buyer would get right now. If there are none you can still buy, it just waits for a seat.

2The room has 67 seats

52 for Twitch15 for YouTube

If one platform can't fill its seats, the other one borrows them. Getting a borrowed seat back:

A new sub or member

Always takes one borrowed seat back.

A new buyer

Only takes it back from someone who isn't active. Active borrowers keep the seat.

3Who sits first

If you're active and already sitting, your seat is yours. Nobody can push you out.

Every other seat is handed out in this order, on each platform:

1
Active subs & members

Chatted, used a command or redeemed something in the last 7 days.

2
Buyers

Bought a plushie with channel points or Cloudbot points. Buying counts as being active.

3
Other subs & members

Haven't been around in the last 7 days. They fill whatever is left.

Inactive people sitting in the room can lose their seat to someone higher on this list.

4Example: the room is full

DAY 1

Shiroko is a sub but hasn't chatted in a whileshe's in the last free seat (group 3)

DAY 2

Momoi buys a plushiebuyers come before inactive subs, so she takes Shiroko's seat

DAY 3

Hoshino, a sub, comes back and chatsMomoi is active and sitting, so she keeps her seat. Hoshino waits

DAY 9

Momoi hasn't chatted for 7 daysshe's not active anymore, so Hoshino (active sub) gets the seat

5Special cases

Bought one, then got a sub or membership?

Your bought plushie turns into your sub/member plushie right away. Same plushie, same seat.

Subbed but the room is full?

You still get your plushie. It waits outside (in the physics room it lands on the floor) until a seat frees up.

Getting a gifted sub

Gives you a plushie, but doesn't make you active. Chat once and you are!

6Commands

!plushiesThe plushies nobody has claimed
!claim namePick or swap your plushie
!myplushWhich one you have, and time left
!seatsSeats you'd get if you sub/join or buy now
!snipe nameWhen a taken plushie frees up
!flipMove your plushie to another seat
The numbers here are the current settings (67 seats, 52 Twitch, 15 YouTube, 7 days) and can change.
